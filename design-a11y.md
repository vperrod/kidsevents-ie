# Small Days colour accessibility

The identity uses dark text on light accents rather than white text on bright colours. The primary teal is dark enough for white button labels.

| Pair | Ratio | Result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Ink `#17312D` on canvas `#F5F7F2` | 12.86:1 | AAA |
| Ink `#17312D` on white `#FFFFFF` | 13.87:1 | AAA |
| White `#FFFFFF` on Atlantic `#1B5C63` | 7.62:1 | AAA |
| Ink `#17312D` on lichen `#DDE8D7` | 10.97:1 | AAA |
| Ink `#17312D` on gorse `#F1C84B` | 8.66:1 | AAA |
| Ink `#17312D` on coral `#E48A78` | 5.42:1 | AA |
| Muted ink `#46615B` on canvas `#F5F7F2` | 6.24:1 | AA |

Coral is not approved as a background for white body text. Gorse is an emphasis surface, never a text colour on white.
